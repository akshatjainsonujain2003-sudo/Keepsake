Good to know

Questions

What is the difference between a Memory Card and a Website?

A Memory Card (₹99) is a single interactive card with your photo gallery, a song, a video and a personal letter. It is great for a birthday or a quick surprise. A Love-Story Website (₹299) is a full multi-section experience (like Wildflower, Red Letter, Mixtape or Sunkissed) with interactive polaroids, wax seals, live relationship timers and multiple “open when” letters.

Does the person need an app to open it?

No. Clicking your link or scanning the QR code opens the Keepsake directly in their phone's browser on both iPhone and Android.

Can I change my photos or message after paying?

Yes! Log into My Account anytime to update photos, swap songs or edit your text. Your existing link and QR code will automatically show the newest version.

Can I keep it private with a password?

Yes. You can add a password when saving your Keepsake. Anyone who scans the QR code or opens the link will need to enter your password before viewing your memories.
