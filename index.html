Good to know

Questions

What is Keepsake?

Keepsake lets you design a digital memory card with photos, a video, a song and a private message, then share it with a single NFC tap or QR scan.

Does the person need an app?

No. Tapping the NFC tag or scanning the QR code opens the card in their phone's browser.

How much does a Keepsake cost?

Designing is free. A template card is ₹49 for 30 days or ₹149 forever; a website is ₹149 for 30 days or ₹399 forever. A 5×7 photo frame with a QR code or NFC chip can be added, and always comes with a forever link. There is no subscription.

What happens after 30 days?

The link pauses and shows a gentle message. Nothing you made is deleted: log in any time and pay the difference to keep it forever, or add another 30 days.

Can I change my card after paying?

Yes. Edit it any time from My memories. The link, QR code and NFC tag keep working and show the latest version.

Can I keep it private?

Yes. Add a password when you save. Anyone who scans the QR code or taps the NFC tag will need it before anything opens.
