Questions

What is Keepsake?

Keepsake lets you design a digital memory card with photos, a video, a song and a private message, then share it with a single NFC tap or QR scan.

Does the person need an app?

No. Tapping the NFC tag or scanning the QR code opens the card in their phone's browser.

Can businesses use Keepsake?

Yes. A business card can show your address on Google Maps, a Google review button, WhatsApp, Instagram, your menu and today's offer.

How do shops log in?

Gift shops and businesses with a Keepsake plan log in with their shop key on the Log in page, then create and manage their cards in the studio.
