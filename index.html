Can I use it as a QR code menu for my restaurant or café?

Yes. Your menu, with sections, prices, photos and bestseller tags, opens when customers scan the QR on the table or counter. Customers can also order on WhatsApp, pay by UPI and leave a Google review from the same page.

Which businesses is it for?

Cafés, restaurants, bakeries, salons, spas, gyms, clinics, dentists, boutiques, kirana stores, CAs, tutors, property dealers and almost any shop or service. Pick your business type and we set up the right layout and wording.

How much does it cost?

₹1,000 once for your page, a printed QR board delivered to your shop and the loyalty stamp card. There's no commission on WhatsApp orders or UPI payments. Keepsake Pro, with the online store and GST bills, is ₹299 a month or ₹2,999 a year, only if you need it.

How does the loyalty card work?

It comes with your page. You choose the reward, like a free coffee after 10 visits or a free head massage after 5. Customers get the card on your page with their mobile number, no app needed. At the counter your staff type a secret PIN on the customer's phone to add a stamp, one stamp a day per customer. When the card is full, the reward is ready. You see every member and can remind them on WhatsApp.

What does the online store add?

Every product with photos, prices and search, and customers order right on your page for pickup or delivery. Orders show up on your dashboard as new, preparing, ready and done. It comes with Keepsake Pro (₹299 a month or ₹2,999 a year). If Pro ends, your products stay on your page and customers order on WhatsApp instead.

What's in Keepsake Pro?

The online store, unlimited GST bills with CA reports, Table QRs, Add sale for walk-in customers, a day-end report with CSV, win-back offers and printable order slips. It's ₹299 for 30 days or ₹2,999 for a year, you renew by hand and there's no auto-debit. We keep adding tools to it.

Do my customers need an app?

No. They scan the QR with their phone camera and your page opens in the browser.

Can I change my menu and prices later?

Yes, as often as you like, from your phone. Log in, edit, tap Save, and the page updates straight away. The QR stays the same.

Where does UPI money go?

Straight to your bank through your own UPI ID. GetKeepsake never touches your money.

Is the printed QR included?

Yes. Your first printed board comes with the ₹1,000 plan. We print your QR on a thick 3.5 × 5 in counter board in the colour you pick and courier it to your shop anywhere in India in 5–7 days. You can also download your QR and print more copies yourself, any time.

I already bought a page. What changes for me?

Nothing is taken away. You keep everything you paid for, at the price you paid.
