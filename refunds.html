Your keepsake goes live the moment payment succeeds, and you can preview and edit everything for free before paying. Because of that, digital purchases are not refundable once activated, except:

  • you were charged twice or charged without your keepsake going live; or

Prepaid QR cards from shops

Cards bought in a shop are refunded or replaced by that shop under its own policy. If your card's code doesn't work or says it is already used, contact us with a photo of the card and your shop bill and we will fix it or issue a new code.

Cancellation

There is nothing to cancel after payment: a keepsake is a one-time purchase with no subscription or auto-renewal.

How refunds are paid

Approved refunds go back to your original payment method through Razorpay within 5 to 7 working days. Your bank may take a few more days to show it.
