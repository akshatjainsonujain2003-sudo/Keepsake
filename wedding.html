Questions

Good to know.

Do guests need an app?

No. The link opens in the browser on any phone. Maps open in Google Maps and replies open in WhatsApp.

Can I change details after sending it?

Yes. Log in, edit and save. Everyone who opens the same link sees the new details straight away.

How do I send a personal link to each family?

Add ?to= and the name at the end of your link, for example ?to=Sharma Family. The invitation then opens with "Dear Sharma Family,". We'll show you this once your link is live.

Where do the RSVPs go?

To the WhatsApp numbers you add, for example one cousin from each side. Each reply says who is coming and how many guests.

Can I still send printed cards?

Of course. Many families print fewer cards for elders and send the link to everyone else. You can also print the link as a QR code on the card.

How long does the link stay live?

It stays live after the wedding too, so you can keep it as a memory. There is no monthly fee.
