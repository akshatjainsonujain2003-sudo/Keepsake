Kya yeh free hai?

Har GetKeepsake page pe pehle 10 GST bill free hain. Uske baad Keepsake Pro: ₹299 mahina ya ₹2,999 saal, jismein online store bhi shaamil hai.

Kya B2B bill (customer ke GSTIN ke saath) ban sakta hai?

Haan. Customer ka GSTIN daalein, state apne aap aa jaati hai aur IGST ya CGST+SGST sahi lagta hai.

Kya jewellery ka 3% GST bhi hai?

Haan. 0%, 3%, 5%, 18% aur 40% sab rates hain.

Kya purchase bill aur e-way bill bhi banta hai?

Abhi nahi. Yeh sales ke GST bill aur CA ke liye sales reports ke liye hai.
