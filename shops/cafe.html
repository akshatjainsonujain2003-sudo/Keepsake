Good to know

Can I use it as a QR code menu on every table?

Yes. Print the same QR for every table. Customers scan it with their phone camera and your menu opens in the browser, no app needed.

Do I pay commission on WhatsApp orders?

No. Orders come straight to your WhatsApp with the items and total. We never take a cut, and UPI money goes straight to your bank.

Can I change prices and items later?

Any time, from your phone. Log in, edit, tap Save. The QR on your tables stays the same.

How does the loyalty card work?

Customers open it from your page and collect one stamp a day when they visit. Your staff add the stamp with a 4-digit PIN. You choose the reward, like a free coffee after 10 visits. It's ₹300 once, so ₹799 with your page.

Is there a monthly fee?

No. ₹499 once and your page stays live. Add the loyalty card for ₹300 and a printed QR for ₹99 if you like.
