Good to know

Questions shop owners ask.

Do my customers need an app?

No. They scan the QR with their phone camera and your page opens in the browser. The loyalty card and offers work the same way.

Is there a monthly fee?

No. You pay ₹499 once and your page stays live. There's no commission on WhatsApp orders or UPI payments.

Can I change my menu and prices later?

Yes, as often as you like, from your phone. The QR stays the same and always opens your latest page.

Where does the money go?

Straight to your bank through your own UPI ID. GetKeepsake never touches your money.

How do Offer QRs on pamphlets work?

Each pamphlet, sticker or ad gets its own QR. Customers claim the offer with their number and show a live green ticket at your counter. You see how many people each one brought in, and you pay only for claims (the first 20 are free).
