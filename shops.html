Questions

Good to know

Can I use it as a QR code menu for my restaurant or café?

Yes. Your menu, with sections, prices, photos and bestseller tags, opens when customers scan the QR on the table or counter. Customers can also order on WhatsApp, pay by UPI and leave a Google review from the same page.

Which businesses is it for?

Cafés, restaurants, bakeries, salons, spas, gyms, clinics, dentists, boutiques, kirana stores, CAs, tutors, property dealers and almost any shop or service. Pick your business type and we set up the right layout and wording.

Do my customers need an app?

No. They scan the QR with their phone camera and your page opens in the browser.

Is there a monthly fee?

No. You pay ₹499 once and your page stays live. There's no commission on WhatsApp orders or UPI payments.

Can I change my menu and prices later?

Yes, as often as you like, from your phone. Log in, edit, tap Save, and the page updates straight away. The QR stays the same.

How do WhatsApp orders work?

Customers tap + on items, then "Order on WhatsApp". WhatsApp opens with the items, quantities and total, ready to send to you.

Where does UPI money go?

Straight to your bank through your own UPI ID. GetKeepsake never touches your money.

I don't have good photos. Will it still look good?

Yes. Every layout is designed to look great without photos, and you can add them any time. A clear photo of your counter or bestseller makes a big difference.

How does the loyalty card work?

It's a ₹300 one-time add-on to your ₹499 page, so ₹799 for both. You choose the reward, like a free coffee after 10 visits or a free head massage after 5. Customers get the card on your page with their mobile number, no app needed. At the counter your staff type a secret PIN on the customer's phone to add a stamp, one stamp a day per customer. The card always shows how many visits they've made and how many are left. When it's full, the reward is ready. You see every member in your studio and can remind them on WhatsApp.

What if I don't have a QR card?

Make your page online and, when you pay, tick "Printed QR, delivered" (₹99 extra). We print your QR on a 3.5 × 5 in counter board in the colour you pick (see the colours) and courier it to your shop anywhere in India in 5–7 days. You can also download your QR and print it yourself.

Can I change my page after the QR is printed?

Yes. The printed QR always opens your latest page. Change your menu, prices, photos or offers any time from your phone: scan your QR while logged in and tap "Edit my page", or open My pages.
