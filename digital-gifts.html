Good to know

Questions

What is a digital gift?

A digital gift is something you give online instead of a physical item. A Keepsake is a personalised website or memory card, with your photos, your song and your words, that they open on their phone.

Is a digital gift too impersonal?

Not when you make it yourself. Everything on a Keepsake comes from you: your photos, the day you met, the song that's 'yours' and a letter in your own words.

How fast can I send it?

Most people finish in about 10 minutes. It goes live the moment you're done, so it works for last-minute birthdays and anniversaries.

Do they need to download an app?

No. It opens in their phone's browser from a link, a QR code scan or an NFC tap.
