Good to know

Questions

What is the best digital gift for a girlfriend?

Something made just for her. A Keepsake website holds your photos, the day you met, your song and a letter, and she opens it on her phone with a link or by scanning a QR code.

Can I send it on WhatsApp?

Yes. You get a link to send on WhatsApp or Instagram, plus a QR code you can print and an NFC link.

Can I keep it private?

Yes. Turn on a password and only someone who knows it can open the page.

Can I edit it after sending?

Yes. Change photos or words any time; the same link shows the latest version.
