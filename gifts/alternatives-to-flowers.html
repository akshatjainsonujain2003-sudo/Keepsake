Make the gift about the person

Pick a photo they love, mention a specific memory, include your shared song, and write a message in your own voice. A digital keepsake is especially useful for long-distance gifting or when you want to send a surprise immediately.

Digital or physical?

Choose a physical bouquet when you want the experience of receiving flowers. Choose a digital keepsake when your goal is to share a personal message, photos and memories that can be reopened later—or when delivery isn't practical.

Questions people ask

What is a good alternative to sending flowers?

A personalised digital memory card or love-story website is an option if you want a gift made from photos, music and your own words.

Can a digital gift feel romantic?

It can when it includes specific memories, your own photos, a shared song and a heartfelt message rather than generic content.

Can I send it when we're in different cities?

Yes. Share the published link through WhatsApp, Instagram or another messaging app.
