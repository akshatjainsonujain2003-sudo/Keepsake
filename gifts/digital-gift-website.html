Questions people ask

What is a personalised digital gift website?

It's a webpage made as a gift, customised with the sender's photos, words, music and memories, then shared as a link.

How much does GetKeepsake cost?

Memory Cards are ₹99 one-time and Love-Story Websites are ₹299 one-time. Design and preview are free.

Can I make a website for my girlfriend or boyfriend?

Yes. You can create a relationship keepsake with photos, music, a letter and design-specific interactive features.

Can the recipient open it without an app?

Yes. It opens in their phone browser from a link, QR code or compatible NFC link.

Can I password-protect the keepsake?

Yes. A password can be enabled so visitors must enter it before viewing.
